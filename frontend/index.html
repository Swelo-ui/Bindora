Introduction & Redocking Standards

In structure-based drug design, the accepted benchmark for validating docking algorithms is crystallographic redocking. A co-crystallized ligand is extracted from an experimentally resolved X-ray complex (RCSB Protein Data Bank), its coordinates are randomized, and it is blindly re-docked into the binding cavity. In accordance with the CASF-2016 benchmark criterion,[2] a heavy-atom Root-Mean-Square Deviation (RMSD) of < 2.0 Å demonstrates research-grade pose-prediction fidelity. Bindora Dock satisfies this requirement across standard Astex Diverse Set targets.

2

Methods: Automated 5-Step Validation Protocol

01

Receptor Ingestion

Raw X-ray structure fetched from RCSB PDB. Multimeric homodimers (Chains A & B) are preserved, maintaining catalytic cleft integrity.

02

Cavity Centering

Native co-crystallized ligand centroid is calculated (e.g. X=13.07, Y=22.47, Z=5.56 for 1HSG) to construct a 20.0–22.0 Å cubic search space.

03

Torsion Prep

Meeko builds flexible torsion trees with Gasteiger charges. Initial dihedral angles are randomized to eliminate crystallographic memory.

04

Monte Carlo Search

Deep Iterated Local Search (AutoDock Vina v1.2.7) explores >4.7 × 106 states per complex under exhaustive global search.

05

Graph RMSD

RDKit symmetry-corrected graph automorphism (AllChem.GetBestRMS) compares heavy-atom poses without rotation penalty.

• Authentic Scientific Transparency: Rigid Pockets vs. Flexible Kinase Flaps

Unlike promotional software demonstrations that present manufactured 100% success claims, real crystallographic docking exhibits known domain-dependent behavior. In enclosed, rigid catalytic cavities (such as 1AQ1 CDK2 at 0.19 Å, 1CX2 COX-2 at 0.76 Å, 1IEP Abl1 at 0.80 Å, and 1HSG HIV-1 at 0.83 Å), Bindora reproduces atomic coordinates with sub-angstrom fidelity. In kinase active sites featuring solvent-exposed flexible loops (EGFR 1M17 and 2ITY), iterated local search identifies near-native poses (2.1–2.9 Å) reflecting conformational sampling challenges recognized in the international CASF-2016 benchmarks.[2]

6. Mathematical & Thermodynamic Equations

Gibbs Free Energy of Binding:
ΔGbind = ΔH − TΔS = RT ln(Kd)
Symmetry-Corrected Heavy-Atom RMSD:
RMSD = √[ (1/N) ∑ minπ ‖xi − yπ(i)‖² ]

Where N represents heavy atom count, and π represents graph automorphism permutations of symmetric chemical moieties (e.g. 180° phenyl flips).

Step-by-Step Computational Pharmacology & 3D Molecular Simulation Manual

Platform Overview

Bindora Dock is an integrated preclinical computational pharmacology suite. It connects native AutoDock Vina molecular docking with RDKit ADME/drug-likeness profiling, EMBL-EBI ChEMBL wet-lab bioactivity cross-checking, and interactive 3Dmol.js WebGL visualization. Follow the 7 tabs below to execute a complete study.

7-Step Preclinical Modeling Workflow

Tab 1: Target & Ligand Preparation Ingestion

• Ligand: Search any drug by name in PubChem, input raw SMILES, or upload .sdf, .mol2, .smi, or .pdbqt files. Conformer generation (ETKDGv3) and Gasteiger partial charges are assigned automatically.
• Receptor: Fetch crystallographic targets from RCSB PDB using a 4-letter ID (e.g., 1CX2, 1IEP) or upload .pdb, .cif, or .pdbqt. The engine automatically removes solvent/crystallographic waters and detects active pocket centroids.

Tab 2: 3D Docking Simulation Scripps Vina

• Verify search space coordinates (Center X, Y, Z and Box Size). Enable the Docking Grid Box checkbox to preview the 3D binding volume.
• Select Exhaustiveness (4 for rapid screening, 8 for academic standard, 16 for publication-grade precision, 32 for deep conformational search / validation) and click Execute 3D Molecular Docking.
• Browse all 9 calculated binding poses with the stepper or click table rows to inspect binding free energy (ΔG in kcal/mol), theoretical Kd, hydrogen bonds, and hydrophobic contacts in real-time.

Tab 3: Pharmacokinetics & ADME RDKit Cheminformatics

• Evaluate the Lipinski Bioavailability Radar Chart covering MW, LogP, H-bond donors/acceptors, rotatable bonds, and TPSA.
• Check GI absorption tier, Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB) permeability (Clark CNS heuristic), Plasma Protein Binding (PPB), and PAINS/Brenk pan-assay interference flags.

Tab 4: ChEMBL Wet-Lab Cross-Check EMBL-EBI REST

• The platform queries EMBL-EBI ChEMBL to check if wet-lab binding assays (IC50, Ki, Kd, EC50) exist for the drug-target pair.
• Note: "Computational Prediction Only" is expected for novel or uncurated compounds—use the "Verify on ChEMBL ↗" links to inspect curated assay archives. UniProt biological signaling pathways are displayed below.

Tabs 5–7: AI Report, Virtual Screening & Dossier Synthesis & Export

• AI Pharmacologist (Tab 5): Generates an automated peer-reviewed scientific narrative detailing mechanism of action and interaction networks.
• Batch Screening (Tab 6): Screen entire compound libraries against the receptor pocket to produce a ranked affinity leaderboard.
• Research Dossier (Tab 7): Consolidates all parameters, thermodynamic constants, and citations into an academic dossier with 1-click PDF export.

How the 3 screening modes interconnect in publication-grade computational drug discovery

The Modern In-Silico Drug Discovery Funnel

Standard molecular docking against a single static receptor typically suffers from a 30–50% false-positive rate because rigid scoring functions reward large hydrophobic surface contacts ("grease balls") regardless of directional chemistry or target flexibility. Bindora resolves this by cascading High-Throughput Scoring → Experimental Pharmacophore Filtering → Conformational Ensemble Validation.

The 3 Pillars of Preclinical Lead Identification Triad Architecture

1 Fast Funnel
Vina & Vinardo
Candidate Screening

Screens multi-compound libraries (SMILES) against the rigid binding pocket. Calculates binding free energy (ΔG in kcal/mol), estimated dissociation constant (Kd), and Ligand Efficiency (LE).

Scientific Role

Ranks hundreds of chemotypes rapidly to eliminate non-binding or sterically clashing compounds.

2 Bio Filter
ChEMBL Actives
Pharmacophore Consensus

Queries curated EMBL-EBI wet-lab binders (IC50 ≤ 10 μM) to extract 3D consensus interaction anchors: Aromatic (π-π), Hydrophobic cores, H-Bond Donors, and Acceptors.

Scientific Role

Eliminates false positives! Ensures hits satisfy the spatial chemistry proven in wet-lab assays.

3 Target Dynamics
Multi-PDB Ensemble
Ensemble Cross-Docking

Cross-docks your prioritized lead across multiple crystallographic structures (apo, holo, mutant variants) to account for receptor conformational flexibility and induced-fit movements.

Scientific Role

Overcomes rigid receptor flaw! Verifies binding resilience across induced-fit conformations.
