Scientific Methodology & Grounded Descriptors:

• Lipinski's Rule of 5: MW ≤ 500 Da, MolLogP ≤ 5.0, H-Bond Donors ≤ 5, H-Bond Acceptors ≤ 10 (Pfizer, 1997).

• Veber Criteria: Rotatable Bonds ≤ 10, Topological Polar Surface Area (TPSA) ≤ 140 Å² (GSK, 2002).

• Pan-Assay Interference (PAINS): Substructure catalog screening against frequent false-positive chemotypes (Baell & Holloway, 2010).

Bindora Dock — User Guide & Research Handbook v1.0

Step-by-Step Computational Pharmacology & 3D Molecular Simulation Manual

⚡ Platform Overview

Bindora Dock is an integrated preclinical computational pharmacology suite. It connects native AutoDock Vina molecular docking with RDKit ADME/drug-likeness profiling, EMBL-EBI ChEMBL wet-lab bioactivity cross-checking, and interactive 3Dmol.js WebGL visualization. Follow the 7 tabs below to execute a complete study.

7-Step Preclinical Modeling Workflow

Tab 1: Target & Ligand Preparation Ingestion

• Ligand: Search any drug by name in PubChem, input raw SMILES, or upload .sdf, .mol2, .smi, or .pdbqt files. Conformer generation (ETKDGv3) and Gasteiger partial charges are assigned automatically.
• Receptor: Fetch crystallographic targets from RCSB PDB using a 4-letter ID (e.g., 1CX2, 1IEP) or upload .pdb, .cif, or .pdbqt. The engine automatically removes solvent/crystallographic waters and detects active pocket centroids.

Tab 2: 3D Docking Simulation Scripps Vina

• Verify search space coordinates (Center X, Y, Z and Box Size). Enable the Docking Grid Box checkbox to preview the 3D binding volume.
• Select Exhaustiveness (4 for rapid screening, 8 for academic standard, 16 for publication-grade precision) and click Execute 3D Molecular Docking.
• Browse all 9 calculated binding poses with the stepper or click table rows to inspect binding free energy (ΔG in kcal/mol), theoretical Kd, hydrogen bonds, and hydrophobic contacts in real-time.

Tab 3: Pharmacokinetics & ADME RDKit Cheminformatics

• Evaluate the Lipinski Bioavailability Radar Chart covering MW, LogP, H-bond donors/acceptors, rotatable bonds, and TPSA.
• Check GI absorption tier, Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB) permeability (Clark CNS heuristic), Plasma Protein Binding (PPB), and PAINS/Brenk pan-assay interference flags.

Tab 4: ChEMBL Wet-Lab Cross-Check EMBL-EBI REST

• The platform queries EMBL-EBI ChEMBL to check if wet-lab binding assays (IC50, Ki, Kd, EC50) exist for the drug-target pair.
• Note: "Computational Prediction Only" is expected for novel or uncurated compounds—use the "Verify on ChEMBL ↗" links to inspect curated assay archives. UniProt biological signaling pathways are displayed below.

Tabs 5–7: AI Report, Virtual Screening & Dossier Synthesis & Export

• AI Pharmacologist (Tab 5): Generates an automated peer-reviewed scientific narrative detailing mechanism of action and interaction networks.
• Batch Screening (Tab 6): Screen entire compound libraries against the receptor pocket to produce a ranked affinity leaderboard.
• Research Dossier (Tab 7): Consolidates all parameters, thermodynamic constants, and citations into an academic dossier with 1-click PDF export.
